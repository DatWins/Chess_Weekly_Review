The Taimanov Sicilian (1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 e6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 Nc6) had a busy week, appearing in both the 41st European Club Championship in Budva and in the Titled Tuesday blitz arena. White tried several systems — the aggressive 6.f4 push, the flexible 6.a3, and the central 6.Be3 — but Black scored convincingly, with Matlakov especially dominant across multiple games.
