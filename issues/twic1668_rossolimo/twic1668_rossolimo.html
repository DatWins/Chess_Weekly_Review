The Nimzovich-Rossolimo (1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5) was a star opening at the 41st European Club Championship in Budva. White scored convincingly with it in several rounds, and even when Black defended correctly the resulting positions proved rich and unbalanced. Wei Yi and Tabatabaei showed that Black can fight back — but it requires precise preparation.
