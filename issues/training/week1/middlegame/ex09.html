Caruana – Moussard · Trompowsky Attack

3rd 3-0 Thursday, Oct 2026 · TWIC 1667 · White to move after 23...Nc3

Position after 23...Nc3 — White to move

Caruana has sacrificed a rook on h6 and the attack is in full swing. Black's king is trapped on h8. What is White's decisive continuation?

A 24. Qd4+ — check with the queen first
Check the king immediately to drive it to g8 before the final combination.
B 24. g5 — close the position
Lock up the kingside to prevent Black's counterplay.
C 24. Nf6! — the decisive knight invasion
Bring the knight to f6 with check, cutting off the king's escape and forcing resignation.
✓ Correct — A is the best plan.
✗ Not quite. The answer is Plan A.

Why Plan A?

24. Qd4+! forces 24...Kh7 (if ...Kg8 then Nf6+ is devastating) 25. Rxh7+ gxh7 26. Qf6 — and the king has no escape from the mating net.

In the game Caruana played the immediate 25. Rxh6! with the same decisive effect — Black resigned on move 28 after 28. Qxf7+. The key theme is the cooperation of queen and knight against the exposed king — a pattern every master knows.

Plan C (Nf6) is tempting but White should check first to drive the king to a worse square. Plan B (g5) is simply wrong — White is winning by force and should not slow down.
