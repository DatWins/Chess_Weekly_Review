Caruana – Moussard · Trompowsky Attack (A45)

3rd 3-0 Thursday, Oct 2026 · TWIC 1667 · White to move after 7...c5

Trompowsky — Black plays ...c5. White to move after 7...c5

Black has challenged with ...c5 in the Trompowsky. The tension in the center is critical. How should White handle it?

A 8. dxc5 — accept the pawn
Take the c5-pawn and try to hold it, putting the onus on Black to win it back.
B 8. c3 — support the d4 pawn
Reinforce d4 and prepare e4 to establish a strong pawn center.
C 8. d5 — close the center
Advance to d5 to gain space and close the center, preparing kingside expansion.
✓ Correct — C is the best plan.
✗ Not quite. The answer is Plan C.

Why Plan C?

8. d5! is the critical move in the Trompowsky — White closes the center and gains a space advantage. After 8...e5 9. Bg3 White has a clear plan of kingside expansion while Black is restricted on the queenside.

In the game, Caruana went on to win brilliantly after 28...Nc3 — a stunning exchange sacrifice on c5 that created a dominant pawn structure. The d5 pawn here was the seed of that winning plan.

Plan A (dxc5) gives Black excellent piece play after ...Bxc5. Plan B (c3) is too passive — it defends without making progress.
